An engineering firm for IT and industrial automation.

Based in Dubai, with an in-house team of engineers and technicians and more than 30 vendor partnerships.

One firm from first survey to handover.

Alif Enterprise works on the IT and industrial automation needs of organisations around the world, and partners with global vendors to bring their technology to local sites.

We deliver end-to-end: enterprise infrastructure, telecommunications, engineering, security and turnkey deployments, plus the software and AI that run on top. One team handles the consulting, design, equipment and installation.

Our delivery model is built to hit quality targets on schedule and at lower cost. That is why enterprise, government and critical-infrastructure clients come back to us.
