More about us

Five steps, one accountable team.

Step 1 of 5

Survey

An engineer walks the site, measures, and records the equipment already in place, so the design starts from facts rather than assumptions.

  • Site visit and measurements
  • Inventory of existing equipment
  • Requirements and constraints agreed
Step 2 of 5

Design

Our engineers size the system for the load you have today and the growth you expect, and show you the cost of each availability option.

  • Solution design and drawings
  • Bill of quantities
  • Options priced side by side
Step 3 of 5

Supply

We order through our 12 registered vendor partnerships, so the hardware is genuine, under warranty and delivered to your site.

  • Sourced through vendor partners
  • Genuine, warrantied hardware
  • Delivered to site
Step 4 of 5

Install

Our own technicians install, cable, test and commission everything, then hand over documentation your team can use.

  • Installation by our technicians
  • Testing and commissioning
  • Handover documents
Step 5 of 5

Support

After go-live we keep the system healthy with maintenance, monitoring and a help desk, and plan upgrades as you grow.

  • Maintenance and monitoring
  • Help desk
  • Upgrades as you grow

Questions we often get.

Something else on your mind? Send it to our sales team and an engineer will answer.

Ask a question

Do you only supply equipment, or install it too?

Both. We handle consulting, design, supply, installation and commissioning with our own engineers and technicians, so one team is responsible from the first survey to handover.

Where is your office?

Our office is at Meydan Grandstand, 6th floor, Meydan Road, Nad Al Sheba, Dubai, U.A.E. Our engineers deliver projects on site, including long-haul fiber along national highways and motorways.

Which brands can you supply?

We are a registered partner of 12 vendors: Cisco, Huawei, Fortinet, H3C, Dell, HP, Lenovo, Samsung, Hikvision, Dahua, Hytera and Motorola.

Do you support systems after go-live?

Yes. Our managed IT services cover monitoring, maintenance and help desk across hardware, cloud and cybersecurity, beyond what a standard SLA covers.

Can you build software as well as infrastructure?

Yes. Our software team builds custom platforms, MVPs and AI solutions, and modernises legacy applications, so the systems we install keep working for your business.

Use the enquiry form or email sales@alifenter.pro or call +971 50 530 1427 with your site location, scope and timeline. We'll come back with a proposed scope and team.
